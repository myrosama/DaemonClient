Changelog

What changed, newest first. Every change people can see gets an entry here, written for the people who use DaemonClient, with a line for contributors where the code moved.

Coming next

  • The iPhone app. A native app for Photos, built on one shared Rust core. Android follows.
  • Faster sync for big libraries. Your cloud will send only what changed since the last sync, instead of the whole library each time.
  • Photos and Drive on the same engine as the apps. The websites move onto the shared core, so a fix lands everywhere at once.

20 November 2026

  • Deleting photos is reliable now. Deleting a lot of photos at once removes every one of them from your Telegram channel, up to 100 messages at a time, instead of stopping partway on Cloudflare's free plan.
  • Deleted photos stay deleted. A deleted photo leaves your library at once and is never shown again. Its record is kept until Telegram confirms the delete, so nothing is left behind if Telegram says no the first time. Restore now says plainly that deleted photos can't be brought back.
  • Old deletion records clean themselves up after 90 days again, and finished deletions no longer sit in Trash.
  • The optional HEIC processor is retired. iPhone photos get their thumbnails from Photos' own Fix HEIC tool, which runs in your browser. If you deployed a processor to your own Vercel account, keep it running for now: clouds that haven't updated yet still call it. This page will say when it's safe to delete.
  • Self-hosting needs Node 22.13 or newer. Node 20 reached end of life in April 2026, and it broke the setup tool's prompts. install.sh fetches a private copy if yours is older.
  • For contributors: the repository is grouped by role: apps/ for what people open, workers/ for the Cloudflare Workers, core/ for the shared engine and packages/ for shared code. Outdated docs were removed.

18 November 2026

  • If your cloud can't get updates, your dashboard tells you. When Cloudflare stops accepting your cloud's authorization, a notice asks you to reconnect Cloudflare. It takes a minute, keeps every file, and must use the same Cloudflare account as before.
  • Updates are safer. Only one update at a time can use your cloud's Cloudflare authorization, so signing in on two devices at once can no longer break it.
  • Ready for the phone apps. Your cloud now stores the preview, photo details and phone-made video copy that the upcoming apps send with each upload.

15 November 2026

  • Drive has a Trash. Files you delete from a mounted drive (WebDAV, in Finder or your file manager) go to Trash instead of disappearing, and uploads through the mount are checked part by part.

14 November 2026

  • New accounts start reliably. Your dashboard waits until your new cloud's address works everywhere before opening it, so a brand-new account no longer gets stuck on "can't reach your cloud".
  • Drive signs you in through your account page. One sign-in, protected by the same human check, for Photos and Drive.

2 November 2026

  • A new Drive landing page, with the product in view from the first screen.

12 October 2026

  • Photos loads what you're looking at first. Thumbnails on screen load before the rest, and the photo you open loads before anything else, so a fast scroll no longer leaves you waiting.

6 October 2026

  • Clouds keep themselves up to date. Fixes now reach every cloud on a schedule, even if its owner hasn't signed in for a while.
  • Each cloud signs its own sessions with its own secret.
  • Photos is lighter. The map and smart search, which never worked here, are hidden, and unused code is gone.
